## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This is authored for this product, not category-interchangeable. The instrument-panel world — night asphalt, headlight amber, confidence rendered as brightness, uncertainty bands drawn under every estimate — is a direct visual translation of the product's core promise ("honest numbers with ranges"). The confidence-as-brightness mapping (bright amber = confident, dim amber = context) is a genuine semantic system, not decoration. A competitor could not lift this look without also stealing the product stance.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI detector is DEGRADED on this machine (htmlparser2/css-select/css-tree/domutils not installed) and fell back to regex matching; it returned 0 findings, which is an undercount, not a clean bill. The in-browser detector (real DOM + computed CSS) reported **19 anti-pattern hits on the dashboard and 42 on the ride detail page**, all from a few families: `undersized-ui-text`/`tiny-text` (9.5–11px functional text — dominant, ~30 hits), `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` (1px border + 30–34px blur, ~10 hits), `dark-glow` (zero-offset colored glows, 3 hits), plus one-offs `border-accent-on-rounded`, `cramped-padding` on the Leaflet attribution/map. It flagged zero contrast violations and zero a11y issues — the color system passes AA.

**Visual overlays:** the bundled browser detector is console-only in this build; findings were logged to the page console but no user-visible overlay was drawn. The page under test is live in the Preview tab.

## Overall Impression

A flagship-grade personal tool hiding behind a micro-type system. The concept, the voice, the honesty mechanics, and the accessibility foundation are all above the bar most commercial products ship; the gaps are execution details — text sized below the legibility floor, floaty card shadows, and a few raw-data leaks (float ticks, "HTTP 500", NaN-tolerant forms). The single biggest opportunity: keep the instrument-panel soul, but rebuild its type scale and surface treatment at a level that reads crafted rather than "AI-dark-theme default."

## What's Working

1. **The honesty system is the product made visible.** Every estimate carries a band and a confidence tag; "brighter means more confident" is the legend under the hero; the cardiac-drift empty state explains *why* it stays quiet ("we can't tell workload apart from wind, so we stay quiet rather than invent a number"). This is design that encodes the brand promise — rare and excellent.
2. **A real, coherent design system.** Tokenized palette, mono tabular data numerals, the headlight rule, the grain, band-draw animation, confidence-coded pills — consistent across all six surfaces. `statCard`, `pill`, and `band` are true components.
3. **Accessibility is baked in, not bolted on.** Skip link, visible focus rings, `aria-current`, keyboard-activatable table rows and dropzone, `aria-live` toast, full `prefers-reduced-motion` support, AA contrast throughout.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] What: Import queue vanishes on navigation.**
- Why it matters: `renderImport` renders an empty `#jobs` and only starts polling after a new drop; navigating away and back mid-import hides running jobs entirely (there's an `/api/jobs` endpoint that is never read). The user can't tell whether the import finished.
- Fix: on `renderImport`, fetch `/api/jobs` and render running/recent jobs; resume polling for any non-terminal job.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] What: Functional text sits below the legibility floor system-wide — 9.5–10.5px labels, tags, chips, readouts, pills, band notes, brand sub, Leaflet attribution.**
- Why it matters: the detector found ~30 instances on the two densest pages. On a 14" laptop these are genuinely hard to read; uppercase letterspacing helps but doesn't save 9.5px. This is the one finding that touches every screen.
- Fix: raise the type floor to 11px (labels/chips), 12px (small body/muted), 12px for the band note; keep the instrument feel through weight, letterspacing and mono, not size.
- Suggested command: /impeccable typeset

**[P1] What: Card elevation is a floaty "GPT shadow" — 1px hairline border + 30–34px blur on every card, instrument, and toast.**
- Why it matters: it's the most recognizable AI-slop signature in the whole design and it undercuts the otherwise crafted night-panel world; the detector flagged it ~10 times.
- Fix: replace the wide blur with a layered elevation (small contact shadow + tighter ambient, or a top-lit edge like the hero's amber rule) so cards read as surfaces, not floating rectangles.
- Suggested command: /impeccable polish

**[P2] What: Raw data and errors leak into the UI.**
- Why it matters: ride-detail chart ticks render float noise (`7.980090000000001`, `8.066666666666666`); the log-axis power-duration chart shows colliding decade ticks; import failures toast "HTTP 500"; profile Save accepts empty fields (NaN payloads); the profile form's value attributes carry float32 artifacts (`16.899999618530273`).
- Fix: format Plotly ticks (1 decimal), constrain `nticks`, map HTTP errors to plain-language messages with a retry hint, validate profile fields inline.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] What: The ride detail page is a long, single-column scroll with everything revealed at once.**
- Why it matters: 8 stat fields, map + scrubber, then 7 chart cards before the drift card; the replay is the delight moment but it's buried mid-page and the gradient-distribution card sits at the bottom.
- Fix: group the charts (elevation/HR/power/speed already pair well in a 2-col grid), consider collapsing the distribution card, and surface the drift finding higher when it exists.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** No keyboard shortcuts anywhere; no search or filter over the rides table (with hundreds of rides this becomes the core pain); no bulk actions beyond multi-file import; compare-two-rides requires drilling in and out of ride pages.

**Sam (accessibility):** The foundation is strong, but the sub-11px micro-type is the barrier — labels, chips, and band notes fail comfortable reading; the map replay is mouse-only (keyboard users can scrub the slider but can't click the map or charts to seek); Plotly SVGs carry no accessible labels.

**Riley (stress tester):** Navigating away mid-import loses the queue view (feature appears to stop working); refresh during import leaves the queue empty; profile form accepts empty/invalid numbers and would save NaN; float noise in chart ticks suggests unformatted data plumbing.

## Minor Observations

- Dark-glow on brand-mark/dot/toast is a deliberate headlight motif, but glow-on-glow (ring + drop shadow on the mark) edges into gaming-RGB; temper the toast glow.
- TSB bars color any negative value red — negative TSB is normal training fatigue, not an error; consider a neutral/amber treatment with red reserved for very negative (< −30).
- Elevation-source pill renders raw backend values (`eudem25m` → "EUDEM25M"); display "25 m DEM" instead.
- "W@140" column header in the route table is jargon; "W @ 140 bpm" reads better.
- The native `confirm()` dialog breaks the crafted world on Delete; a styled inline confirm (or undo toast) would match.
- Dashboard renders its charts without skeletons — cards flash empty while six endpoints resolve.
- Readout grid stays at 4 columns below 1100px and never collapses further, cramping 7 fields on narrow windows.
- "Import queue" card shows no empty-state hint when idle.
- Power duration curves log axis shows two decades of 1–10 ticks colliding ("8 9 1 2 3 4…").

## Questions to Consider

- The single-ride dashboard shows four legend entries for HR levels that only one has data for — what would the "confident" version of a sparse chart look like?
- Is the ride-detail page a reading experience or a playground? It currently tries to be both, top to bottom.
- If the micro-type were the only thing between this and "commercial flagship", why not fix it tonight?
